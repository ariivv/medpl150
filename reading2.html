Reading 2: ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster - Nesrine Malik

Malik suggests that humans are being consumed by AI. The use of AI has been on the rise for the past few years, but it has recently gotten out of hand. This new technology can create hyper realistic images which make it hard for us to decipher whether things are real or fiction. This has raised concerns about the use of this technology going towards creating political propaganda. Since AI images and videos are fairly simple and quick to create, it’s easier for governments to release many of these forms of media in a fast fashion; in other words, easier to spread propaganda. Since this form of content is produced using prompts and existing content, AI can create very controversial and bias pieces of media. Malik even goes on to describing this as “the new aesthetic of fascism”, because AI will create visually appealing images that promote discriminatory ideas. I also agree with her claim that this form of technology has unfortunately desensitized people when it comes to global and political conflicts. AI artist will make memes out of real-world issues which lead to consumers becoming numb towards these problems. Aside from Malik’s claims, I believe AI can also create global issues between countries, especially if a majority of people can’t tell if what they are consuming is fiction. This new era of technology has become more than an easy way to create content, and I believe will cause dangers and excess conflicts if we continue down this path. With the worlds current status, a compilation of a few AI posts can stir up chaos within and throughout politics.
